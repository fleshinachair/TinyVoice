# Resynth

## Resynth (the third personality, 2026-11-24)

`engine` = Resynth (`VT_ENGINE_RESYNTH`, run mode `VT_MODE_RESYNTH` = `VT_MODE_COUNT + 1`). A sound modelled on the reference plugin, **measured** against the real plugin (TinyVoice-lab `calibration/FINDINGS.md` has every law). Vocoder and Voice FX are bit-identical whatever the Resynth knobs say (tested).

**Engine (`hm.c`).** Per hop (256 samples): pitch by `cher_mpm` on a 2x-decimated 1536-sample frame (Range = `min_f0`/`max_f0`), a pitch-adaptive Hann window (4 periods) zero-padded to a 4096 FFT, harmonics by peak interpolation (their common deviation removed: only inharmonicity is kept), residual noise in 24 bands read at the leakage nulls between harmonics, an envelope on a 160-point log grid. Tuner: Scale (the 12-bit mask), Note (+Duo), MIDI (the newest two held pads; none = silence); the deviation from the target is high-passed (Quantize, Flatten x Robotify: the higher cutoff wins; cutoffs solved for this hop-rate one-pole). Lines per target: main, Note Shift (scale-following; both lines -3 dB), Octave down/up. Per partial: formant-shifted envelope (+Sharpen: a fixed curve plus 21 % tilt flattening per unit, both loudness-neutral), Emphasis (levels pulled 20 % toward the loudest), Transform (dB morph toward the wavetable, weight t^1.7, power kept), the spectral Filter (near brick-wall HPF/LPF, resonance in dB, Gaussian mid band), and Synth Mix as two paths (A the voice, B the resynthesis; weights (1-sm)^0.65 / sm^0.65 at a 123 -> 90 degree phase as Transform grows). Synthesis: inverse FFT (1024 frame, Blackman-Harris kernel, triangle overlap-add over 2 hops) plus band noise (sine-windowed 512 IFFT). +2.3 dB makeup on the output, dry included. Latency Lo/Mid/Hi = 1024/1280/2304 samples (the reference's: ~38/85/181 ms; ours is lower on purpose, for live singing). Wavetable: one cycle, Stretch (a phase knee over the table's own samples) and Shape (Chebyshev coefficients moved from the identity toward the preset's) before its FFT.

**Measured fit** (TinyVoice-lab `ab_*.py`): tuning exact; vibrato kept 75/39/12/3 % vs 79/41/13/3; harmonic levels (Robotify, Emphasis, Transform, Shape, Stretch 0.1-1.0) within ~1 dB; Formant within ~2-3 dB; Sharpen ~2 dB; HNR within 1.4 dB; Widen within ~1 dB; filters (edges, resonance, mid band) within 1-3 dB; Note Shift lines within 0.3 dB. 40 factory presets x 3 real vocals (vocadito; v9, after the gate / 256 harmonics / short-table / Stretch laws; the reference's reference the loudest of four renders): 1/3-octave spectrum distance 2.2 dB mean, 1.9 median; the audible score (each band floored 50 dB under its render's loudest) 2.0 mean, 1.8 median; level error 1.2 dB mean, 0.9 median (was 4.1 / 3.0 / 10.4 before the 2026-11-24 Opus pass; 2.5 / 2.1 / 1.4 after it). Worst left: Sad Cyborg (5.5), Big Computer (3.7), Rotten Robotics (3.1); Drunken Organist went 6.4 -> 2.1 and Fourier's Bassliner 3.5 -> 2.2 with the Stretch law. The resonance peak's width was measured: within ~1.5 dB of the reference's. **All of it is general engine laws measured on test signals; nothing is per preset.** Known gaps: the octave lines where their partials coincide with the main line's (the reference cancels or adds depending on the amount; not modelled), Synth Mix 0.5 on one pitch (the reference's interference varies with pitch: modelled as one average phase), Warble's fast mode at rate 1, notes below Range Lo (the reference plays them ~47 c sharp; we tune them). The reference offline sometimes renders a preset silent or half-built (a start-up race): the lab retries.

**Laws found in the Opus pass (2026-11-24).** Note Shift **transposes** path B (not an extra line); Synth Mix is a linear crossfade; Octave lines follow the shifted pitch; every line holds the voice's loudness wherever it is moved (the reference is the voice's harmonic series); Stretch is a phase knee over the table's own samples (before the zero padding) at (s, 0.5) up to 0.5 and (s, 1 - s) above, saturating at 0.9 (fitted on six tables 0.2-0.9 within 0.25-0.6 dB; the first fit, (1 - s, 1 - 2 (1 - s)^2) over the padded cycle, matched only the saw and was 3-10 dB off every other table above 0.5); HPF / LPF are brick walls, negative resonance a roll-off from 1.4 octaves inside, positive a peak just inside the edge, also with the filter at its end stop; mid-band sigma = 0.01645 x Width octaves; Out Gain stops at +9 dB and scales the wet only (the dry gets the +2.3 dB makeup); a -0.5 dBFS output limiter; a wavetable is played **zero-padded to max(256, next power of two) samples** as one cycle (a 92-sample library table is 92 samples of wave then 164 zeros: much brighter than its own spectrum; within 0.3 dB per harmonic on five library tables; 107 of the 282 presets carry such a table, the named Saw / Square / Sine / Triangle are 4096 and unaffected), applied where a table becomes our 512-sample `hm_wt` (`import_humanoid.py`, the Remote UI's .baby import) and in `hm_set_wavetable` for a raw cycle. The tracker searches from an octave under Range Lo (58 Hz at least) to 1.6 kHz: a baritone under C3 (the INIT Range Lo) was silent 68 % of a phrase, now 14 % like Humanoid; this is likely much of "cuts out sometimes" for low voices on the Move.

**Laws found later the same day.** (1) **Gate** (the reference's own, `hm.c`; not the module's Mic Gate in front of it): the RMS, not the peak (a mean square with a ~20 ms one-pole against the threshold squared; a sine and a voice open at exactly RMS = threshold), Attack the opening fade and Release the closing one (linear; they were swapped), Hold exact, after In Gain. Ours was a peak gate that stayed open ~8 dB under the threshold on a voice. Tested (`test_resynth_gate_rms`). (2) **256 harmonics** (`HM_KMAX`, was 160): the reference plays every harmonic to k = 256 and none above, so a line under ~112 Hz (low voices, Shift -12, Octave down) had lost its top (at 61.7 Hz everything over 9.9 kHz). (3) **Short tables play a flat block**: a table of N samples zero-padded to P = max(256, next power of two) has harmonic k < N at |FFT_P[k]| / N and **every harmonic N <= k < P at amplitude 1** (a gain table left at 1 past the table's length, it seems), nothing above P; measured on 92 / 184 / 225-sample library tables, every harmonic to 256 within ~1 dB, Stretch and Shape included. It matters only for tables of up to 256 samples (57 presets with Transform on). A 92-sample table therefore buzzes flat from harmonic 92: Drunken Organist (POLY-6 PW, Shift -12) is a 5-6.4 kHz buzz in the reference, and was 19-24 dB too loud from 500 Hz to 4 kHz in ours because Transform keeps the voice's power. `build_wavetable` builds such a table at its padded length (the law needs the exact FFT) and a full cycle at 2048 as before; `hm_set_wavetable` records the length, `hm_set_wavetable_len` sets it for a cycle that was padded and resampled already (the module's `hm_wt`). Tested (`test_resynth_wavetable_short`, `test_resynth_wt_len`). Not modelled: Transform 1 on a low line is 2-3.5 dB quieter in the reference at ~60 Hz (0.7 at 82 Hz, ~0 from 130 Hz; Auto Level evens it out on the Move).

**In the module (`dsp.c`).** Params `hm_<Humanoid id>` (64, from `hm_params.h`), then `hm_key`, `hm_scale` (Major, Minor, Chromatic, Custom = `hm_mask`), `hm_mask`, `hm_wave` (Saw, Square, Triangle, Sine, Preset = `hm_wt`). `hm_wt` is not a chain param: a string (512 int16 samples, base64, then optionally `:<N>`, the Humanoid table's own length before padding, 2..4096, written by `import_humanoid.py` and the Remote UI's import; without it the cycle is a plain full cycle, so older imports load unchanged but miss the short-table block: re-import them), saved in `state`, parsed without the 31-character value limit. `hm_freeze` (Glitch) is transient. Resynth runs with Auto, Tone, Spread, Mix and Level out of it (its own Gate, Widen, Dry/Wet, Output) and under the -6.1 dBFS headroom stage; pads feed MIDI mode (`hm_sync`, per block); `hm_begin` once a block, `hm_tick` per sample. Pages: Main (Transform, Robotify, Formant, Synth Mix, Shift, Octave, Quantize, Dry/Wet), Pitch (+ Smoothing), Synth (Transform again, then Wave, Shape, Stretch, Warble x 3, Widen: the wave next to the knob it acts through), Tone, Play, Notes; everything else on the Remote UI. **N/A**: a Resynth knob that does nothing in the current settings shows a dimmed "N/A" on the Move (`custom:tinyvoice_rsna`, 17 knobs; Latch's widget follows the same rule in Resynth) and is dimmed on the Remote UI: Shift / Octave / Transform at Synth Mix 0 (path B), Wave / Shape / Stretch also at Transform 0, Odd/Even at Shape 0, Key / Scale / the keyboard in Note mode (and MIDI with No Pad Silent; with Bypass only a shifted line uses them), Duo / Note 1 outside Note mode, Note 2 also with Duo off, No Pad and Latch outside MIDI mode, Warble Rate at Depth 0 (Mode also with a synced rate), Mid Freq / Width at Mid Gain 0, Out Gain with Auto Level on, Glitch Div with sync off (Length with it on), the Gate's timings with the Gate off, De-ess timings at 0, Widen's at 0, Sharpen Bias at 0. **The rules are one block of text, `RS_ACTS`, identical in `canvas.js` and `web_ui.html`** (the canvas test compares them); the widget's `extra_keys` are the keys a rule reads (`RS_DEPS`, the generator's copy checked against module.json). Unlike the vocoder's N/A knobs, the DSP still takes writes to them: imports and restores write keys one at a time in any order. **Where a knob acts, the widget draws the host's own stock picture** (2026-11-24; it used to print the value, so Mid Freq's dial became a number): canvas.js `import * as HOST_RENDER from "/data/UserData/schwung/shared/param_pages/render_page_movy.mjs"` (the import other modules' ui.js make; the widget and page loaders evaluate canvas.js as an ES module) and `drawStock` asks `widgetKindFor(meta)` with the host's `metaIndex` for the key: `drawArcKnob` (a range), `drawEnumSquare` (an option), `drawBigNumber` (a small whole-number range: Shift), placed as `drawKnobWidget` places them. The vocoder's Key / Scale / Retune / Glide / Beauty widgets do the same. With no metadata, a missing export or a throw it prints the value as before; if the host ever moves that file the whole canvas.js fails to load (stock dials everywhere, no scope). `tests/canvas_test.mjs` swaps the import line for a switchable stub; checked once against the real host renderer copied from the Move. `chain_params` is now 11.5 KB (the host's limit is 64 KB, chain_params + ui_hierarchy).

**Presets page (2026-11-24).** The `resynth` level carries the browser triple (`rs_preset` / `rs_preset_count` / `rs_preset_name`), so the host draws **Presets** as Resynth's first page (scroll = load). **The page's body is ours** (2026-11-24): chain_params ends with `rs_presets_page`, a `"type": "canvas"` entry with `as_page` + `preset_browser` (listed in the resynth level's params; not a DSP param), so the host plans it as *the* level's preset page (one page, knobs still live, browsing still the host's) and calls `canvas_overlay.drawPage` in canvas.js with `payload.preset` = {name, index, count, entered} in a 120 x 45 frame: the name word-wrapped over up to three lines (".." past that), "12 of 61" under it. The stock page showed one line in a big face and cut most imported names (282, 212 over 20 letters, the longest 44). Verified with the host's own `page_plan.mjs`; no other module on the device used the feature yet, so it is unseen there. A canvas preset page also runs the host's knob rotation, whose extra stop reads `preset_name` and writes the header's name, while the browser lane writes `rs_preset_name` there: unanswered, the header flickered between the preset and the module name (device report). So `preset_name` answers the same string as `rs_preset_name` while the engine is Resynth (and is unanswered in the other engines, as before); the header therefore names the preset on Resynth's knob pages and in the chain editor too (`test_resynth_preset_name_header`). The list is `rsbank.c`: every `.json` in `<schwung>/presets/tinyvoice/` whose state has `engine` Resynth (state stored as an object or a string; other modules, Vocoder / Voice FX states and broken files skipped), sorted case-insensitively by name like My Presets. `plugin.c` reads it on a thread of its own at `create_instance` (the instance is a `vt_inst` wrapper holding that thread; destroy joins it) and hands it over once with `dsp_set_rs_bank` (atomic release / acquire; `rs_preset_name` reads `(loading)` until then, `(no presets)` for none). Choosing one resets every saved Resynth param and the wavetable to defaults, then applies the preset's state (`rs_preset` is transient). **The page's place survives a reload** (a deploy restarts Schwung, and the page used to come back on Default's first preset, whose neighbour the next detent then loaded over your sound): `state` carries `rs_bank` and `rs_name` (the preset last loaded, by name: indexes move when presets are added); restored before the bank arrives, the index is pointed back at that name once it is in (`rs_resolve`, at the next block or browser read), nothing reloaded. A bank switched by hand lands on the loaded preset if that bank lists it. A preset's own `rs_bank` / `rs_name` (one saved from My Presets) never moves the bank being browsed. Tested (`test_resynth_bank_place`). Presets saved after TinyVoice loaded appear the next time it loads. On the Move: 282 presets ready before the first UI read, worst load 0.38 ms on the audio thread (`device_smoke.c`). This build needs glibc 2.34 (`pthread_create`; the Move has 2.35).

**No live waveform on the Remote UI (2026-11-24, measured; dropped at the user's request).** A LIVE panel (waveform, pitch trail, meters) was built and removed. The manager's iframe `getParam` answers from a cache, not the device; the cache holds only the keys the manager fetches (the non-transient members of `state`, the preset browser's list / count / name keys, and the `extra_keys` of the viz widgets: that is how `scope` arrives, and why `hm_wt` does), and a `subscribe` re-reads them from the device **at most about once every 2.5 s** (the manager ignores the ones in between; measured with a websocket client: subscribe, change a value through `schwung-testd`, watch `param_update`). A waveform needs ~10 Hz. Only a direct stream from the module (its own listening port) would do it, which exposes the mic's waveform to the network; not done. The engine feed built for it was removed. If live feedback comes back, the Move's OLED is the place (the viz widget is read at ~5 Hz by the host; Resynth now fills `viz_f0` / `viz_target` / `viz_cents` for it).

**Synth Voice (renamed from Voice FX, 2026-11-24).** The second engine is shown as **Synth Voice** (engine option, `modes` entry and level key `"synth voice"`: the host builds the Mode page's labels from the level names, so the level carries the display name; verified by running the host's `page_plan.mjs` on `module.json`). Internal names are unchanged (`vf_*`, `VT_ENGINE_VOICEFX`, `vfx.c`, the prose below still says Voice FX), and `dsp_set_param("engine", "VoiceFX")` is accepted as the old name, so sets and My Presets saved before the rename load.

**Recorded Demo voice (2026-11-24).** The Demo plays a real sung phrase when the module folder has `demo_voice.raw` (raw s16le mono 44.1 kHz, 7.4 s, excerpt of vocadito recording 25, CC BY 4.0: credits in `CREDITS.md`, shipped as `CREDITS.txt`, and in the Demo help page; `scripts/make_demo_voice.py` cuts it and level-matches it to the synthetic voice, `src/demo/demo_voice.raw`). `plugin.c`'s loader thread reads it (`demo_pcm_load`) and hands it over once (`dsp_set_demo_pcm`); with none the synthetic phrase plays as before. Voice FX still uses its groove. Not yet heard: pick another clip with the script if this one doesn't suit.

**Banks, Hold Scale, Auto Level, Demo (2026-11-24).** (1) The Presets page has two banks, chosen on the **Bank** page (`rs_bank`, transient: Default / Imported; the Remote UI header has the same switch). **Default** = the 30 presets shipped in the module folder (`src/presets/*.json`, copied to `<module>/presets/` by `build.sh`; our own, generated by TinyVoice-lab `calibration/make_default_bank.py`, which also writes five of its own wavetables); **Imported** = the user's Schwung preset folder (imported Humanoid presets, anything saved from My Presets); then Imported **split by kind** (2026-11-24, 282 presets were too many to scroll): **Tuner, Voco, Harmony, Synth, Dryish, Instrument** (Humanoid's Factory categories: 61 / 39 / 28 / 16 / 18 / 18), **Artists** (the anniversary pack, 101) and **Mine** (anything else: your own saves). A preset's kind is its file's top-level `category` (written by `import_humanoid.py`; INIT gets `Init`, listed in Imported only), else its name's Factory prefix ("1. Tuner - ..."), else Mine (`rsbank_kind`); the split banks share Imported's states (`RSBANK_NAMES` in `rsbank.h` is the option list, in order). The Remote UI's bank switch is a dropdown. `rsbank_load(default_dir, imported_dir)`; plugin.c's loader thread passes `<module_dir>/presets` and the user folder. (2) **Hold Scale** (`hm_scale_hold`, On by default, Pitch page): a preset (Presets page, or a state set after the first second) leaves `hm_key` / `hm_scale` / `hm_mask` alone; the restore at load still sets them; a state saying Hold Scale Off applies in full. (3) **Resynth runs under Auto** (input AGC +12 dB max, voice gate): the presets' gate thresholds (-20..-50 dB) and the tracker expect a DAW-level voice, and on the Move's quiet mic 34 presets dropped out or never started. **Mic Gate** (`hm_mic_gate`, -72..-24 dB, default -42, Play page and the Remote UI's Output panel; saved, but a preset load leaves it alone like Auto Level) is that voice gate's threshold in Resynth (the vocoder's MORE Gate knob does nothing there): a soft voice under -42 dBFS RMS was taken 40 dB down with no knob to fix it (tested: a -50 dBFS voice -79 dB out at -42, -17 dB at -60). The room on this Move measured -45..-54 dBFS, so under about -54 the room opens it. (4) **Auto Level** (`hm_autolevel`, On, Remote UI): an output leveller in `hm_tick` (slow 250 ms RMS reference that cannot jump more than 6 dB a step, held in pauses, aim -17 dBFS RMS, gain -12..+27 dB) followed by a fast peak limiter (ceiling 0.4955 = -6.1 dBFS, the headroom stage's; 0.4 ms attack; it was 0.30 = -10.5 dBFS and -18 dBFS until 2026-11-25, which left Resynth ~3 dB under Robot with its peaks 10 dB lower); Out Gain feeds the leveller, so a preset's own loudness trim is levelled away (32 imported presets carry one, Zardonic's at -12 dB, which applied after the leveller made them 12 dB quiet; the Remote UI dims Out Gain while Auto Level is on); the Default bank lands -16.1..-18.7 dBFS RMS (mean -17.2; Robot -17.4) on three vocadito phrases, the imported presets -17.4..-20.1 (p90..p10), peaks -6.5 dBFS (`TinyVoice-lab calibration/level_engines.py`, `level_imported.py`): the 282 imported presets' sung level spread went from 20 dB to 8 dB (p10-p90), and the first half second of a note no longer overshoots 20 dB (the cause of 'Opera Singer clips'). The A/B tools leave it off (`hm_init`). (5) **Resynth Demo** steps five Default presets (`k_rs_demo` in dsp.c: Tune - Hard Snap, Voco - Saw Choir, Harm - Fifth Above, Synth - Bell Tone, Fx - Dark Choir), 4 bars each, snapshots the player's Resynth sound first and restores it at the end; a hand on a Resynth knob or the Presets page pins the tour. (6) Wave, Stretch, Shape and Odd/Even act only through Transform (the Remote UI dims them at 0). (7) Latency moved to the Play page; the Pitch page holds Hold Scale; Duo is the first knob of the Notes page, with Note 1 / Note 2 (it was on Pitch). Humanoid's MIDI mode plays the **two newest held pads whether or not Duo is on** (Duo only adds the second fixed note in Note mode); TinyVoice matches that.

**Your own banks** (added 2026-11-25): made, filled and removed from the Remote UI, per engine; see `docs/mangler.md`, "Your own banks".

**Presets.** Schwung User Presets (My Presets): `{"name","module":"tinyvoice","version":1,"state":"<state JSON>"}`; imported ones carry their mask and wavetable, state 2.6-2.9 KB. Import with `scripts/import_humanoid.py` + `deploy_humanoid_presets.sh`, or drop a .baby on the Remote UI (then save on the Move).

**CPU.** Native x86, heaviest settings (Duo, Shift, Octave 1.5, Transform, every effect, Hi): 0.3-0.4 ms per 128-frame block (test_resynth_cpu checks < 0.6). On the Move (`device_smoke.c`, fails over 2.0 ms): Resynth 0.58 ms, Transform 0.53, heaviest 0.65 ms per block.

**Calibration harness facts.** the reference renders headless in DawDreamer only with the Win32 message loop pumped (`hum_host.pump`): without it the wavetable synth is silent. Trial silence is per instance in rendered samples (60-65 s, 120-125 s); renders stay under 20 s on fresh instances. A MIDI note in the first ~0.05 s is dropped. Presets load as plugin state (`juce_state.py`).
